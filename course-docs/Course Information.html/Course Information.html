3. Tutorials

There will be weekly tutorials held on Thursdays and Fridays at 2:00 pm and 3:00 pm on each day, starting in the first week of term. Each student must attend one tutorial a week (i.e. you will sign up for either a Thursday or Friday slot). The sign-ups are available on Amathuba. If you need to sign up for a certain time slot, but it is full, please email the course convener ASAP.

For advice on tutorials, please see this page.

Attendance

  • Attendance is mandatory for DP and will be recorded each week. 
  • Please bring your student card (or even a photo of it on your phone) to every tutorial as we will be using those to take attendance.
  • Students are expected to attend for the full duration of the tutorial (45 minutes). Arriving excessively late may result in you not getting attendance that week.
  • If you miss a tutorial but want to make it up, you are free to attend a different slot. The list of tutorial slots (including locations and times) can be found under the Tutorials tab on Amathuba. Outside of this, there is no way to "make up" a tutorial, you will just be marked as absent for that week.
  • Please track your own attendance--do not email your tutor or lecturer asking how many tutorials you have missed.

Missing Tutorials

Though students are expected to attend every tutorial, students may be granted a maximum of 2 permitted absences without it affecting DP. This includes missing tutorials due to illness, travel, other obligations, etc. You do not need to email anyone (course convener, lecturer, tutor, etc.) regarding tutorial absences.

Should you be unable to attend your usual tutorial slot, you are welcome to attend another tutorial slot the same week in order to "make up" the absence (see above).

If you miss 3 or more tutorials, you will be refused DP. Please plan your semester carefully. If there are extenuating circumstances, these can be addressed in a DP appeal at the end of the semester.

4. Self-Assessment Quizzes

Roughly every two weeks, there will be online Self-Assessment Quizzes run on Amathuba. These are intended to provide you with an opportunity to check that you are understanding the work and to motivate keeping up to-to-date with content. 

Students are expected to complete these Self-Assessment Quizzes individually, but using notes, tutorials and other resources is permissible. Any work you submit must be your own. If you use external resources or check your answers with classmates, you are expected to still be able to explain and understand the answers you submit. The use of generative AI for these quizzes is discouraged.

Announcements will be posted indicating when each quiz becomes available. Each quiz will have a strict deadline and no extensions will be granted.

5. Tests

There will be two in-person, invigilated, closed-book class tests during the semester:

  • Class Test 1 is on Monday 24 August from 6:00 pm to 7:30 pm
  • Class Test 2 is on Tuesday 6 October from 6:00 pm to 7:30 pm

Students are expected to write both class tests. Venues and syllabi for each test will be announced closer to the time. Clashes will be addressed closer to the time.

Extra Time/Special Concessions

If you require extra time or other concessions for tests, please submit your letter from the UCT Disability Service here on Amathuba.

Medical Certificates

Should you miss a test due to illness, you must submit a medical certificate to the course convener within 5 calendar days of the test. Note that it is departmental policy that “as I was informed” medical notes will not be accepted. This means that you must physically (in-person) obtain a medical note (telephonic or online consultations are not valid). Furthermore, the medical note should be on or before the day of the test in question (not the day after or even later!). If you do not submit a valid medical note, you will be marked as absent for the test and receive a mark of 0.

If submit a valid medical certificate and your absence is accepted, it is possible that an alternate arrangement will be made in order to assess your knowledge of the content. This is at the discretion of the course convenor. For example, you may have to do an oral test or written test to make up for the missed one. Details will be shared directly with the students concerned.

6. Duly Performed Certificates

In order to write the final exam, you must earn a duly performed (DP) certificate. This will be granted if (and only if 😉) you have met all of the following requirements at the end of the semester:

  1. Have full attendance of tutorials (with up to a maximum of 2 permitted absences, see above).
  2. Wrote both class tests.
  3. Have a test average of at least 30%.

If you fail to meet any of these requirements, you will be refused DP and will be unable to write the exam.

7. Exam

There will be a final exam written during the exam period at the end of the semester. The date and venues will be announced by the UCT Examinations Office during the semester.

The supplementary/deferred exam will be written in January 2027. The date and venue will be communicated to the relevant students by the UCT Examinations Office. In order to qualify for a supplementary exam, you must (1) be granted DP, (2) write the November exam and (3) have a final mark between 45% and 49% after the November exam. This cannot be negotiated. The process for awarding supplementary exams is automatic. 

8. Marks

Class record \(C\) will be calculated using the following formula (where \( T_1 \) is your mark for Class Test 1 and \( T_2 \) is your mark for Class Test 2 and \(S\) is your Self-Assessment Quiz average):

\[ C = 0.475 T_1 + 0.475 T_2 + 0.05 S \]

Final marks \(F\) will be calculated using your class record \(C\) and examination mark \(E\) as follows:

\[ F = \mathrm{max}\{0.8 E + 0.2 C, \, 0.6E + 0.4C \} \]
