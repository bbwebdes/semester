Class tests:

There will be two invigilated class tests. The provisional dates are:

Class Test 1: 2 September, Venue and time TBC
Class Test 2: 7 October, Venue and time TBC

The exact dates, venues and times will be announced in due course, please keep an eye on the announcements.  If you think there may be a clash with any of your other tests, please let the course convenor know as soon as possible.

If you are ill on the day of the test, you will need to submit a  medical certificate within a week from the day of the test.  The medical certificate should clearly state your full name, the medical professional's details, and must state specifically that you were examined by the doctor, confirming that you were not well enough on the day of the test to write the test. Medical certificates based on remote consultations, or stating “as I was informed..'' (and similar) will not be accepted.

Please note: If you miss a test without a legitimate reason, and do not submit a medical certificate within a week from the day of the test, you will get 0 for that test.

If you miss one test and provide a medical certificate (as indicated above), then you will be excused from the test. In that case, your class record be calculated using only the mark you obtained in the other test (see the formula for the class record and for the final mark, below).

If you miss both class tests for a legitimate reason, you will be required to write an alternate written test or an oral test, as decided by the course convenor.


Class record:

Your Class  Record (CR) will be calculated using the following formula:

$$\text{CR}=0.5 \text{T1} + 0.5 \text{T2}$$

Where T1 denotes your mark for Test 1, and T2 denotes your mark for Test 2.


DP and exams: 

There will be a two hour invigilated exam at the end of the semester.

The exact dates and venues for the exams will be communicated in due course.


A Duly Performed Certificate (DP) is needed in order to be allowed to write the examination.  If you do not get a DP, you cannot write the exam, and you therefore cannot pass the course.

To obtain a DP for the Exam, you will need:

A Class Record of 20% or higher and have attended 80% of your tutorials/workshops.


Final mark: 

Your Final Mark for MAM2013S (2IA) will be calculated using the following formula:

$$\text{FM} = \text{Max}\{0.6\text{E}+0.4\text{CR}, 0.8\text{E} + 0.2\text{CR}\}$$

where FM denotes your final course mark, E denotes your mark for Exam Paper and CR denotes the class record (see class record above).

If you are ill and cannot write an examination, you need to apply to the Deferred Examinations Committee of UCT for permission to write a deferred exam.  The course convenor is allowed to excuse you from a test, but she is NOT allowed to excuse you from the exam.  That gets done by the Deferred Exams Committee. Please DO NOT email the course convenor asking to allow you to defer the exam.

Note that the deferred examination will only be written in January 2027.
